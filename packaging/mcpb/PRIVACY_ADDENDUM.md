## Local desktop extensions (Cinvar Project Inspector)

The Cinvar Project Inspector runs entirely on your computer inside Claude Desktop. It reads the single project folder you
choose and returns project details (asset names, durations and sizes, timeline structure, captions, validation issues, the
folder's local path, and your machine's video-encoder support and free disk and memory) to Claude in your conversation, under
your Claude account's own terms and privacy policy. It does not edit or write to your project, does not make network requests,
does not send data to ELYRAON, has no telemetry, and needs no ELYRAON account. Remove the extension in Claude Desktop to stop
all access. Questions: colt@elyraon.com.co.
