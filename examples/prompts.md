# CINVAR example prompts

Natural-language instructions the harness understands. Replace paths with your own
media. The agent turns these into validated timeline operations.

## Basic editing
- Combine the clips in ./footage, add a title, insert transitions, normalize the audio, and export a 1080p video.
- Cut the first 3 seconds of every clip, crossfade them, and put my logo in the corner.

## Revisions
- Shorten the intro to two seconds, make the title larger, and remove the second clip.
- The pacing drags in the middle. Cut 20% out and make the transitions smoother.
- Revert to the version before the last change and try a warmer grade.

## Short form
- Make a vertical Short from this horizontal clip with readable captions.
- Find the three best moments in this podcast and make three vertical clips with captions.

## Commercial
- Create a 30-second commercial using my product photos, with price cards at the end.

## Documentary
- Make this footage look like a professional documentary: slow pacing, restrained grade, no music stings.

## 3D (requires Blender)
- Use Blender to create a 3D animated intro, then add it to the start of my video.

## Analysis-driven
- Tell me which shots are usable (no black frames, no clipping), then build the best 15-second cut.
- Detect the silent gaps in the interview and remove them without cutting words.
