# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want CINVAR to be a place where anyone can contribute the quality of their work
without being asked to justify their identity, background or experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people.
- Critique the artefact, not the author.
- Assume the other person has information you do not have, then ask for it.
- Accept that "this does not work yet" is a legitimate contribution.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing private information,
or sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone is
representing the project. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may restrict
participation, in proportion to the behaviour. Enforcement decisions are made by the
maintainers and are not subject to public debate with the reported party.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately.
